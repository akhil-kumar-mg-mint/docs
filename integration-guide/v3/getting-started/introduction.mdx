## What you can collect for

Every payment has a **purpose code** that says what the money is for. The purpose code decides who is allowed to pay, which documents you need, and what tax applies.

  <Card title="Education fees" icon="graduation-cap" href="/integration-guide/v3/web-integration/lrs-compliance">

## The rules you are working within

EximPe is licensed by the RBI as a **Payment Aggregator – Cross Border (PA-CB)**. A partner bank makes the actual transfer abroad. Four things shape what you can build:

- **₹25,00,000** is the most one payment can be.
- **Every payment needs a purpose code**, from the set your account is enabled for.
- **Every payment needs documents.** Which ones depends on the purpose code.
- **Some payments attract tax at source.** For education, tax starts only after the buyer has sent ₹10,00,000 abroad in a financial year.
