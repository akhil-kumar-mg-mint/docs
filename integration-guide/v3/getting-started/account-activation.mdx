Sandbox works the moment you sign up. To take real money you need to finish KYC and be approved.

Start it from your [EximPe Dashboard](https://merchant.eximpe.com) — click **Complete KYC**.

## Documents to prepare

Gather these before you start. Missing any of them stops your activation.

**For your authorised signatory**

- Board Resolution, signed and sealed, authorising them to act
- PEP / KMP declaration
- Passport copy
- Address proof

**For your business**

- Business Registration or Trade Licence
- Articles of Association (MOA / AOA)
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Tax Registration Certificate
- Tax Residency Certificate
- Bank statement, last 3 months
- No Permanent Establishment declaration
- UBO declaration

<Note>
  Depending on your tax jurisdiction we may also ask for **Form 10F** or a **withholding tax declaration**. These are not always required.
</Note>

## What happens after you submit

| Status | What it means | What you do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `PENDING` | You have not started KYC | Start it |
| `IN_PROGRESS` | Partly filled, not submitted | Finish and submit |
| `SUBMITTED` | With our team for review | Wait |
| `RESUBMIT` | We need something changed or re-uploaded | Fix what we flagged, submit again |
| `APPROVED` | KYC passed | Your account is activated for live payments |
| `REJECTED` | We could not approve the application | Talk to your EximPe contact |

## API keys

**Sandbox keys** appear as soon as you sign up at [merchant-uat.eximpe.com](https://merchant-uat.eximpe.com/). Use them to build and test against simulated money.

**Production keys** appear only after your account is approved and activated at [merchant.eximpe.com](https://merchant.eximpe.com/).

<Tip>
  Switch both your keys **and** your endpoint when you go live. Sandbox keys do not work against the production API.
</Tip>
