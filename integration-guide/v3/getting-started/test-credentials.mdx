## Where to find your keys

Open the **Developer** section of the [EximPe Dashboard](https://merchant.eximpe.com). You get:

- **Client ID**
- **Client Secret**

Sandbox keys appear as soon as you sign up. Production keys appear once your account is activated.

<Warning>
  Sandbox and production keys are not interchangeable, and neither are the two hosts. Going live means changing **both**.
</Warning>

## Environments

| | Base URL | Dashboard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sandbox** | `https://api-pacb-uat.eximpe.com` | [merchant-uat.eximpe.com](https://merchant-uat.eximpe.com) |
| **Production** | `https://api-pacb.eximpe.com` | [merchant.eximpe.com](https://merchant.eximpe.com) |

Build and test against sandbox. Nothing there moves real money, and you can [simulate an incoming transfer](/integration-guide/v3/web-integration/virtual-accounts#step-4-test-it-without-a-real-transfer) instead of making one.

## Headers on every call

| Header | Who sends it | What it is |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `X-Client-ID` | Everyone | Your Client ID |
| `X-Client-Secret` | Everyone | Your Client Secret |
| `X-Merchant-ID` | PSPs only | The sub-merchant the call is for |
| `X-API-Version` | Everyone | `3.0.0` |

```bash
curl -X POST 'https://api-pacb-uat.eximpe.com/pg/vba/' \
  -H 'X-Client-ID: <client_id>' \
  -H 'X-Client-Secret: <client_secret>' \
  -H 'X-Merchant-ID: <sub_merchant_id>' \
  -H 'X-API-Version: 3.0.0' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{}'
```

<Warning>
  **`X-API-Version` is not optional.** Leave it out and the call fails with a `401` whose message talks about credentials — which sends you hunting through your keys instead of your headers.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Keep your Client Secret on your server. It is not safe in a browser or a mobile app.
</Note>

## If you are a PSP

You call the API on behalf of the businesses you onboard, so there is one extra step before your first call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the merchant">
    In sandbox a dummy merchant is created for you at signup. Otherwise see [Adding Merchants](./adding-merchants).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for activation">
    Sandbox activates instantly. In production it follows due diligence under RBI rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use their merchant ID">
    Once active you get a `merchant_id`. Send it as `X-Merchant-ID` on every call you make for that sub-merchant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A PSP cannot collect for a sub-merchant until that sub-merchant is active.
</Note>
