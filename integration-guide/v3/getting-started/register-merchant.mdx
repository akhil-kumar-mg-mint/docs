<Tip>
  Sign up on **Sandbox** to start building. Sign up on **Production** separately to submit KYC — the two can happen at the same time.
</Tip>

1. **Navigate to the sign-up page**
   - For **Production**: [https://merchant.eximpe.com/auth/sign-up](https://merchant.eximpe.com/auth/sign-up)
   - For **Sandbox**: [https://merchant-uat.eximpe.com/auth/sign-up](https://merchant-uat.eximpe.com/auth/sign-up)
<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/sign_up.png"
    alt="Collect Payments from India with Ease"
    style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  />
</Frame>
2. **Enter your business email**
3. **Verify your email using the OTP we send**
4. **Fill in your company info:**
   -  Business Name
   -  Country
5. **Choose Account Type:**
   - **Merchant** — you collect for your own business
   - **PSP** — you collect on behalf of other businesses
6. **Done**


<Info>
You'll land on your EximPe Dashboard — activate your account and start integrating from there.
</Info>
