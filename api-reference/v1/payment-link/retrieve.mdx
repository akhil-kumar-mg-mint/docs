title: 'Retrieve Payment Link'
openapi: 'GET /pg/payment-links/{paymentLinkId}/'
---

## Overview

Fetch detailed information about a specific payment link using its unique ID. This includes the current status, the payments made through it, buyer details, and associated order information.

## Path Parameters

- `paymentLinkId`: The link's `link_id` from [Create Payment Link](/api-reference/v1/payment-link/create) (e.g., `PR5106085554`). The `payment_id` it returns is the same value.

## How the link was paid

A buyer may try more than once before a payment goes through. Two fields show the payments made through the link:

- `paid_payment_id`: The `payment_id` of the payment that paid the link, or `null` until it is paid. Refund and reconcile by this ID.
- `payments`: The payments made through the link, newest first — at most the 50 newest. Each entry carries the payment's `payment_id`, its `status` (`pending`, `captured`, `failed` or, rarely, `invalid`), its `mop_type` (e.g. `upi`, or `null`), its `amount` in the format Create Payment Link returns (`"1500"`, or `"1500.50"` when there are paise), `created_at`, and `payment_completed_at` (`null` until it completes).

On some accounts a link and its payment share one ID: `payments` then holds a single entry whose `payment_id` is `link_id`, from the moment the link is created. Elsewhere each try is a payment of its own, and `payments` stays empty until the buyer first tries to pay.

`link_id` is the link's ID, and `payment_id` is the same value, kept for compatibility. See [Tracking a payment link](/integration-guide/v1/web-integration/payment-links#tracking-a-payment-link).
