<Note>
  The request and response are the same whatever `X-API-Version` you send. v1, v2 and v3 integrations share these chargeback endpoints.
</Note>

## How answering works

1. Call this endpoint for the chargeback.
2. Pick an **action** (`accept`, `contest` or `partial_accept`) and one of its **conditions**.
3. Send that condition's fields to [Respond to Chargeback](/api-reference/v3/chargebacks/respond), each as `fields[<name>]`.

Every chargeback on your payments can be answered here, whichever payment gateway the payment went through. The response shape is always the same, but what is on offer depends on the payment. You will see one of two sets:

- **Accept, or contest with evidence.** `accept` has a single `accepted` condition that takes nothing. `contest` has a single `contested` condition: your `remarks` (required) and any of the evidence documents, all optional, with `min_documents: 1`. Attach whichever documents back your case, and at least one.
- **Detailed conditions.** Each condition describes the situation that applies, such as `product_delivered` or `already_refunded`, and takes its own text fields, true/false fields and documents. `partial_accept` may be offered too.

Read the conditions from here for each chargeback instead of hard-coding them.

`actions` is empty, and `can_respond` is `false`, while your answer is under review or once the chargeback is closed. If EximPe sends the chargeback back to you for more information, its status becomes `INSUFFICIENT_DOCUMENT` and its actions are offered again.

<Note>
  `403` means the chargeback can't be answered through EximPe yet because its gateway credentials are not set up for your account. Contact EximPe support; retrying won't change it.
</Note>
