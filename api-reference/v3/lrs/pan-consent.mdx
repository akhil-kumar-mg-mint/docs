title: 'Verify a PAN and Record Consent'
description: "Verify a buyer's PAN and record their consent before you create a Hosted Checkout order, so the buyer skips the PAN page."
openapi: 'POST /pg/pan/consent/'
---

## How it works

1. Show the buyer the consent statement below and get their agreement.
2. Call this endpoint with the buyer's `pan` and `buyer_pan_consent: true`.
3. If `verified` is `true`, create the order within 24 hours on [Create Order](/api-reference/v3/order/create#skip-the-pan-page). Send the same PAN as `buyer.pan_number`, with `business_model` and `purpose_code`.

If `verified` is `false`, no consent is recorded. The buyer enters their PAN on the PAN page in Hosted Checkout.

## Consent

Send `buyer_pan_consent: true` only after the buyer has agreed to the statement Hosted Checkout shows on its PAN page.

- **LRS payments:** "By continuing, I confirm that the PAN I entered belongs to the person paying for this purchase, that I have permission to share it, and that this payment is within their LRS limit for this financial year, and I agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy."
- **Other consumer payments:** "By continuing, I confirm that the PAN I entered belongs to the person paying for this purchase, that I have permission to share it, and I agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy."

The consent lasts 24 hours and covers every order created with that PAN in that time. After 24 hours the buyer sees the PAN page again; call this endpoint again to renew it. For an LRS order, the time of this call is recorded as the buyer's LRS declaration time.

## Reasons

| `reason` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `INVALID_PAN` | The PAN does not exist. |
| `NON_INDIVIDUAL_PAN` | The PAN belongs to a company, firm or other non-individual. |
| `INOPERATIVE_PAN` | The PAN is inoperative. |
| `AADHAAR_NOT_LINKED` | The PAN is active but not linked to Aadhaar. |

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `40357` | `buyer_pan_consent` is missing or not `true`, or `pan` is missing or badly formed. `details` is keyed by the field. |
| `503` | `40356` | Verification is temporarily unavailable. No consent was recorded. Retry after the `Retry-After` header (60 seconds). |
| `500` | `40356` | Anything unexpected. No consent was recorded. |

{
  "success": false,
