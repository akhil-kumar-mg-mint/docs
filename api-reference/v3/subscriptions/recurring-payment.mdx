---
title: 'Trigger Recurring Payment'
description: 'Charge a subscription installment. How the charge runs depends on your payment partner.'
api: 'POST /pg/subscriptions/{subscription_id}/recurring_payment/'
---

Charge an active subscription. What you send, and when, depends on your payment partner. See [How charging works by payment partner](/integration-guide/v3/web-integration/manage-subscriptions#how-charging-works-by-payment-partner).

- **Cashfree**: `ADHOC` subscriptions only. Plans with any other `billing_cycle` are charged by Cashfree, and calling this endpoint for one returns `400`. The debit runs the next day (IST). The order is created when the outcome webhook arrives.
- **PayU** and **EximPe**: every billing cycle, `ADHOC` included. First send a [pre-debit notification](/api-reference/v3/subscriptions/pre-debit-notification), then call this endpoint on the notified `debit_date` (IST). The order is created at once and its `order_id` is returned.

<Warning>
  **Confirm every charge by webhook.** Always confirm the charge outcome from the [Payment Successful](/api-reference/v3/webhooks/payment-successful) or [Payment Failed](/api-reference/v3/webhooks/payment-failed) webhook before marking an installment paid.
</Warning>

<Info>
On PayU and EximPe, only one charge can be in progress at a time: while one is pending, a new call returns `409`. Each pre-debit notification allows up to 3 failed attempts; after that, send a new notification. On Cashfree, one charge can be set per debit date.
</Info>

<Info>
A `409` means the subscription's state refused the charge: no pre-debit notification for today, a charge already in progress, or an inactive mandate. A `400` means a field is invalid, the subscription is a Cashfree plan that is not `ADHOC`, or PayU or EximPe declined the charge. A `403` means your merchant account is suspended or closed. A `502` means the payment partner could not be reached, or Cashfree refused the charge.
</Info>

### Path Parameters

<ParamField path="subscription_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier (uid) of the subscription.
</ParamField>

### Request Body

<ParamField body="amount" type="number">
  Amount to charge, up to 12 digits with 2 decimals, minimum `0.01`. Must not exceed the subscription's `billing_amount` or the amount in today's pre-debit notification. If you leave it out: Cashfree and PayU charge `billing_amount`, and EximPe charges the notified amount.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="invoice_display_number" type="string">
  **Required on PayU.** Up to 255 characters. Send the same value you sent in today's pre-debit notification. Optional on EximPe: if you send it, it must match the notification's value (the notification response returns it). Not used on Cashfree. On PayU, use a new value for each charge. A value already used on another of your orders is refused with `400`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="transaction_id" type="string">
  Up to 64 characters. **EximPe:** letters, numbers and hyphens only; this becomes the charge order's `reference_id`, and it must be unique among your orders. If you leave it out, one is generated with a `REC-` prefix. **PayU:** optional; identifies the charge with PayU. Not used on Cashfree.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="reference_id" type="string">
  Cashfree and PayU. 1–50 letters, numbers and hyphens, unique across your orders. **Cashfree:** it becomes the debit's `payment_id`. **PayU:** it becomes the charge order's `reference_id`. If omitted, one is generated with a `REC-` prefix. **EximPe:** not used. Send `transaction_id` instead.
</ParamField>

### Response Fields

<ResponseField name="subscription_id" type="string">
  Cashfree. The subscription identifier.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="payment_id" type="string">
  Cashfree. Identifier of this debit: the `reference_id` you sent, or one generated with a `REC-` prefix. Store it against your order: when the debit's outcome arrives, its payment carries this value as `merchant_reference_id` in the [Payment Successful](/api-reference/v3/webhooks/payment-successful) and [Payment Failed](/api-reference/v3/webhooks/payment-failed) webhooks, and as `reference_id` in [Get Subscription Payments](/api-reference/v3/subscriptions/payments). A retried debit has `-attempt-1`, `-attempt-2`, and so on appended. It is not the ID of a payment record, so [Get Payment](/api-reference/v3/payment/get) does not accept it.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="payment_amount" type="number">
  Cashfree. The amount charged for this recurring payment.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="order_id" type="string">
  PayU and EximPe. The charge order's identifier. Track it with [Get Order Status](/api-reference/v3/order/get-status).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="reference_id" type="string">
  EximPe. The charge order's `reference_id`: the `transaction_id` you sent, or one generated with a `REC-` prefix.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  All partners. `success` or `pending`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="message" type="string">
  All partners. A short description of the result.
</ResponseField>
