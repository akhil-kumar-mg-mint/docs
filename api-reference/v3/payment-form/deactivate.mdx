---
title: 'Deactivate Payment Form'
description: 'Close a payment form so its public page stops accepting payment, while everything collected through it stays on the record.'
openapi: 'POST /pg/forms/{uid}/inactivate/'
---

## Overview

```bash
curl -X POST 'https://api-pacb-uat.eximpe.com/pg/forms/PF4829301756/inactivate/' \
  -H 'X-Client-ID: <client_id>' -H 'X-Client-Secret: <client_secret>' \
  -H 'X-API-Version: 3.0.0'
```

The page at `/pay/<url_slug>/` stops accepting payment, the form leaves the [list](/api-reference/v3/payment-form/list), and the response is the form as it now stands with `is_valid: false`.

Nothing is deleted. Payments already taken keep their orders, their settlements and their refund path, and the form stays on the record for reporting.

<Warning>
  **This is one-way over the API.** Every Payment Forms action resolves the form through a queryset filtered to active forms, so once deactivated the form answers `404 ERR_FORM_001` to retrieve, to update, and to the `is_active: true` that would bring it back — the revival request never reaches the field it would set. To pause a form you intend to reopen, set `valid_until` with [Update](/api-reference/v3/payment-form/update) instead.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **`valid_until` is the reversible alternative.** Outside its `valid_from` / `valid_until` window a form `404`s the payer at `/pay/<url_slug>/` exactly as a deactivated one does — but it stays listable, retrievable and patchable, so moving the date reopens it. Deactivation closes the page *and* the API.
</Note>

<Note>
  **A deactivated merchant account has the same effect on every form it owns.** The public page checks that the collecting account is active as well as the form, so closing or suspending an account `404`s all of its forms for payers at once, without deactivating any of them.
</Note>

<Note>
  **The slug is not released.** Creating a new form on the same `url_slug` is refused with `ERR_FORM_002`. Pick a fresh slug for the replacement.
</Note>

<Note>
  **`DELETE /pg/forms/{uid}/` does the same thing.** It is a soft delete — it sets the form inactive and returns `{"success": true, "message": "Payment form deleted successfully", "data": {}}`. Prefer this endpoint: it says what it does and hands back the resulting form.
</Note>

<Note>
  **`X-Merchant-ID` is not read on this endpoint.** The form is reached by its `form_id`, scoped to the account your credentials identify. It is required only on [create](/api-reference/v3/payment-form/create) and optional on [list](/api-reference/v3/payment-form/list).
</Note>
